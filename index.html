01 - ABOUT ME

Building a future,
one skill at a time.

Building is not just what I study.
I have always been fascinated by structures and curious about how ideas become designs, and how those designs become the structures that shape the world around us.

This is the main reason I chose to pursue a degree in Civil Engineering Technology. However, Engineering is more than designing structures. It's about understanding problems, thinking critically, and having the ability to create solutions that improve the way people live.

To be better equipped, I decided to learn programming, web development, CAD, AI, and other digital tools alongside my engineering studies. These skills allow me to approach engineering from different perspectives.

My long-term vision is to combine engineering, technology and entrepreneurship to build meaningful projects and develop practical, economical, and reliable solutions. Eventually, I hope to establish a construction company of my own.

Aside from engineering and chasing my goals, I love listening to music, reading books, sharing deep thoughts and meaningful conversations, and exploring random topics that catch my interest. There is still a lot I am learning about myself — what I value, and what kind of person I want to become.
